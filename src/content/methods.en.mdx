<h2 id="hypothesis">Hypothesis</h2>

The quantitative question and the outcome that would refute it are defined before the calculation. The criterion is recorded to avoid retrospective changes in interpretation.

<h2 id="data">Data</h2>

Tests use public data sets (BAO, supernovae, cosmic chronometers, the CMB, astronomical catalogues). Each result identifies the dataset, its version and selection where this information is available in the public record.

<h2 id="model">Modelling</h2>

The reference model is flat ΛCDM, with expansion

<Tex display tex={String.raw`E^2(z)=\frac{H^2(z)}{H_0^2}=\Omega_m(1+z)^3+1-\Omega_m`} />

and the most common extension for evolving dark energy, the CPL parametrisation,

<Tex display tex={String.raw`w(a)=w_0+w_a(1-a),\qquad \frac{\rho_{\rm DE}(z)}{\rho_{\rm DE,0}}=(1+z)^{3(1+w_0+w_a)}\exp\!\left(-\frac{3w_a z}{1+z}\right)`} />

Each test specifies its free and fixed parameters, along with their priors. A fixed calibration that absorbs part of the signal is treated as a model assumption and tested separately.

<h2 id="tests">Tests</h2>

The fit improvement of a model over the null hypothesis is measured by

<Tex display tex={String.raw`\Delta\chi^2=\chi^2_{\Lambda{\rm CDM}}-\chi^2_{\rm model}`} />

and, when nested sampling is used, by the Bayes factor

<Tex display tex={String.raw`\ln B=\ln Z_{\rm model}-\ln Z_{\Lambda{\rm CDM}}`} />

Partial estimates of Bayesian evidence cannot be compared across runs. Comparisons require completed calculations using the same data, priors and likelihood. Convergence checks, independent seeds and limiting cases precede the recording of results.

<h2 id="review">Review</h2>

Review seeks to refute the result by identifying unstated assumptions, missing reference models, unjustified causal interpretations or numerical artefacts. Objections must be addressed before approval.

<h2 id="result">Result</h2>

A result is described with its value, the test conditions and its scientific status. The five statuses are:

- **Candidate:** internal result without independent validation yet.
- **Testing:** under verification by additional tests.
- **Validated:** reproduced and reviewed within the project criteria.
- **Refuted:** contradicted by a later test.
- **Published:** described in an authorised publication.

Inclusion in the catalogue does not imply validation. Each change in status is recorded in the public approvals manifest in the repository.

<h2 id="publication">Publication</h2>

Papers, notes and preprints are made public after approval, together with their editorial status. DOIs, arXiv identifiers and references are checked against their sources where available.

<h2 id="repro">Reproducibility</h2>

- Result identifiers are derived from the text of the public question and stay stable while the question does not change.
- The Data page identifies the public catalogue release (Tower) and its SHA-256.
- If Tower is unavailable, the last validated catalogue release remains available.
- Internal Tower records are neither read nor published by this site.
