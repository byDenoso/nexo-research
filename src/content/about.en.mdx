NEXO is an independent research system in computational and observational cosmology, developed under the direction of Dener Pereira, at the Federal University of Rio de Janeiro.

<h2>Mission</h2>

To test cosmological models against public data with criteria written before the result, and to publish each result together with its status and its limits. The work centres on the expansion of the Universe and dark energy, and extends to alternative models, perturbations, high-redshift objects, Local Group dynamics and catalogue analysis.

<h2>How the work is organised</h2>

The research is computational. Hypotheses, data, models, tests and reviews are recorded in the Tower, the operational base of NEXO. Computational agents run and verify calculations under explicit rules, and the scientific decision stays with the researcher. This site shows only content cleared for release.

<h2>Who this site is for</h2>

Researchers and prospective collaborators find the methods, data and conditions of each test. Editors find the real editorial state of each piece of work. Journalists and readers without a physics background find, on every result, the question investigated and a plain-language interpretation when one is available.
