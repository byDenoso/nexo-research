<h2 id="mission">Mission</h2>

NEXO (**Nature, Exploration & Origins**) is an independent cosmology research initiative. We test hypotheses against public data. Released results include the conditions and limitations of the analysis so that other researchers can repeat the calculations.

<h2 id="work">What we do</h2>

Research on fundamental physics and the dark sector includes tests of dark-energy models using supernovae, baryon acoustic oscillations (BAO) and the cosmic microwave background (CMB). Observational data and simulations are used to compare competing hypotheses. One control in expansion tests, for example, is to repeat the fit after excluding a redshift range from the sample.

The research also covers cosmic evolution, structure growth and the earliest galaxies, including the selection of distant sources in astronomical catalogues.

<h2 id="conduct">How the research is conducted</h2>

The criterion of each test is set before the calculation. Input files are identified by hash. In catalogue searches, part of the sample is held out and used only once, after the analysis specification has been fixed. Calculations undergo automated checks; result approval requires review by someone who did not produce it.

The validation rules are published under [Code](/en/code). The procedures are described in [Methods](/en/methods).

<h2 id="integrity">Integrity</h2>

- Null and refuted results stay in the catalogue, with the same prominence as the others.
- The status of a result changes only by a recorded editorial decision, visible in the repository history.
- Fields not confirmed against the source are left empty.
- Third-party data are cited through the original publication of the collaboration responsible.
- Errors that are pointed out are corrected in the record itself, with the date of the correction.

<h2 id="cite">How to cite</h2>

Cite a result by its record identifier and the date of the catalogue release. The citation, including the file hash, is under [Data and sources](/en/data#cite).

<h2 id="collaborate">How to propose a check</h2>

To propose a check of a fit, sample cut or hypothesis, include the record identifier when writing to the address under [Contact](/en/contact). Independent checks are documented with the names of those responsible.
