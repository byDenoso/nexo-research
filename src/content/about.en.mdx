<h2 id="mission">Mission</h2>

NEXO Research Institute tests hypotheses in cosmology against public data and publishes each test with its conditions and limits. The aim is that someone else, with the same data, can repeat the calculation and reach the same number.

<h2 id="work">What we do</h2>

In the expansion tests, we compare dark energy models using data from supernovae, baryon acoustic oscillations and the cosmic microwave background. For example, we repeat a fit after removing one redshift range from the sample to measure how that choice affects the result. Other research areas cover structure growth and the selection of distant sources in catalogues.

<h2 id="conduct">How the research is conducted</h2>

The criterion of each test is set before the calculation. Input files are identified by hash. In catalogue searches, part of the sample is held out and used only once, after the analysis specification is frozen. Calculations run in a computational pipeline with automated checks, and a result moves forward only after review by someone who did not produce it.

The validation rules are published under [Code](/en/code). The full path of a test is described in [Methods](/en/methods).

<h2 id="integrity">Integrity</h2>

- Null and refuted results stay in the catalogue, with the same prominence as the others.
- The status of a result changes only by a recorded editorial decision, visible in the repository history.
- Fields not confirmed against the source are left empty. Nothing is inferred.
- Third-party data are cited through the original publication of the collaboration responsible.
- Errors that are pointed out are corrected in the record itself, with the date of the correction.

<h2 id="cite">How to cite</h2>

Cite a result by its record identifier and the date of the catalogue snapshot. A ready reference, with the file hash, is under [Data and sources](/en/data#cite).

<h2 id="collaborate">How to propose a check</h2>

Use the record identifier to say which fit, sample cut or hypothesis you want to verify, and write to the address under [Contact](/en/contact). Independent checks enter the record, with the name of whoever made them.
