NEXO Research is an independent programme in computational and observational cosmology. Its central question is how cosmic expansion, dark energy and structure growth can be understood from public astronomical observations.

<h2>Scientific mission</h2>

Develop and test cosmological hypotheses through transparent analyses. Each public result identifies its physical question, test conditions, scientific status, limitations and, where available, data sources, code and reproducibility materials.

The programme focuses on cosmic expansion and dark energy. Related research examines consistency across probes, theoretical models, distant galaxies, exotic sources and catalogue anomalies.

<h2>Research approach</h2>

Hypotheses are specified before calculation and confronted with reference models. The process records data choices, parameters, priors, sensitivity tests and attempts at refutation. The Tower organises internal records; this site publishes only content cleared for public release.

Automation executes and checks computational tasks. Physical interpretation, editorial decisions and scientific responsibility remain with the researcher.

<h2>Scientific direction</h2>

NEXO is developed under the scientific direction of **Dener Pereira**, whose stated academic affiliation is the Federal University of Rio de Janeiro (UFRJ). This portal claims no team, institutional partnership or endorsement from external collaborations.

<h2>Collaboration and public access</h2>

Researchers can inspect questions, methods, sources and limitations and propose independent checks. Candidate results, verified analyses and publications are distinct categories; changes in status are never automatic.
