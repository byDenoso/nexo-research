NEXO displays the supplied declination and redshift subset of BGS_BRIGHT-21.5
NGC/SGC clustering catalogs (v1.5), in Cartesian comoving coordinates. The web
distribution separates the incoming little-endian position and redshift
records into contiguous binary files, preserving the supplied bytes.
