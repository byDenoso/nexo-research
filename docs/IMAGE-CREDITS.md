# Observational image attributions

The following external observations illustrate research topics on the NEXO website.
They are not NEXO findings, are not claimed to have been used as NEXO inputs,
and do not imply institutional affiliation or endorsement.

## JWST Advanced Deep Extragalactic Survey (JADES)

ESA/Webb images are released under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence.
The files in `public/images/` are crops of the ESA/Webb "publication JPEG" files, resized and
re-encoded; nothing else was changed. The credit is shown next to each image with a link to the source.
