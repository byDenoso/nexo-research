# Image attributions

The following external observations illustrate research topics on the NEXO website.
They are not NEXO findings, are not claimed to have been used as NEXO inputs,
and do not imply institutional affiliation or endorsement.

## JWST Advanced Deep Extragalactic Survey (JADES)

ESA/Webb images are released under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence.
The files in `public/images/` are crops of the ESA/Webb "publication JPEG" files, resized and
re-encoded; nothing else was changed. The credit is shown next to each image with a link to the source.

| File | Source | Credit | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| `jades-gs-z14-0-field.jpg` | [JADES-GS-z14-0 environment (NIRCam image)](https://esawebb.org/images/jades5/) | NASA, ESA, CSA, J. Olmsted (STScI), S. Carniani (Scuola Normale Superiore), JADES Collaboration | 3:2 crop of the lower 68%, 3200 × 2133 px |
| `jades-transients.jpg` | [Transients in JADES field](https://esawebb.org/images/JADES7/) | NASA, ESA, CSA, STScI | 4:5 crop, 1400 × 1750 px. The 83 circles are part of the source image |
| `jades-goods-south.jpg` | [GOODS-S field (NIRCam image)](https://esawebb.org/images/JADES2/) | NASA, ESA, CSA, B. Robertson (UC Santa Cruz), B. Johnson (Center for Astrophysics, Harvard & Smithsonian), S. Tacchella (University of Cambridge), M. Rieke (Univ. of Arizona), D. Eisenstein (Center for Astrophysics, Harvard & Smithsonian), A. Pagan (STScI) | 4:5 crop of the centre, 1400 × 1750 px |

The social card reuses `jades-gs-z14-0-field.jpg` with a display crop and a dark overlay.
The credit and source address appear on the card.
